## Conflict rule

A conflict between a derived document and the authoritative assignment blocks
affected implementation. Conflict resolution requires:

1. identification of the exact conflict;
2. preservation of the assignment requirement;
3. a proposed, documented resolution; and
4. updates to the decision log and affected specifications.

These steps are prerequisites to resuming affected implementation.
