# Code of conduct

OVTK welcomes contributions and discussion from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone participating in this repository is expected to help keep it respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with patience, clear reasoning, and constructive feedback.
- Respect differing experiences, identities, and perspectives.
- Accept corrections, acknowledge mistakes, and focus on resolving the problem.
- Keep issue reports and reviews relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy and use synthetic examples rather than confidential operational data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Repeated disruption, spam, or retaliation against someone raising a concern is also unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. The repository maintainer, [Cobblecat](https://github.com/Cobblecat), may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, restrict participation, or use GitHub's moderation tools as needed. Responses should reflect the severity and context of the conduct.

## Reporting a concern

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** tools for abusive content or accounts. For project moderation, contact Cobblecat through a private contact method made available on the profile. If no private contact method is available, open an issue asking for one without naming people or posting sensitive details.

Do not publish private evidence, personal information, or allegations in a public issue. Reports will be handled with care, and retaliation against a person raising a concern is prohibited.
